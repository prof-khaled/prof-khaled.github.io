# Quality assurance report

## Academic checks
- 16 modules are present and follow a coherent prerequisite sequence from experimental foundations to advanced quantum chemistry.
- Every module contains rationale, learning outcomes, substantive topic explanations, equations, at least one scientific visual, a worked example, practical activity, misconceptions, exam guidance, mastery feedback and source notes.
- Reproduced uploaded figures are attributed by source and page/figure number where visible.
- Original plots and diagrams are explicitly described as theoretical/educational rather than measured data.
- External web sources are restricted to official university benchmarking and NIST/CODATA constants.

## Accessibility and technical checks
- English/Arabic toggle; Arabic content is right-to-left.
- Semantic headings, alt text on figures, responsive layout, keyboard-accessible details controls, print stylesheet, reduced-motion handling.
- Local CSS/JavaScript/assets are packaged; the core course remains readable without third-party scripts.
- Search, bookmarks and progress use local browser storage only.

## Administrative caveat
Course code, credit/contact hours, institution, programme and instructor remain provisional — institutional verification required.
