# International benchmarking note

Access date: 7 August 2026.

The course was benchmarked against official offerings rather than copied from one programme. MIT OpenCourseWare 8.04 Quantum Physics I organizes an undergraduate first course around experimental foundations, wave mechanics, the Schrödinger equation, one-dimensional potentials, angular momentum and hydrogen. University of Sydney PHYS2012 (2026) explicitly includes Stern–Gerlach spin, quantum measurement, time evolution and a computational physics laboratory. University of Cape Town PHY2004W includes assumptions of quantum mechanics, wavefunctions/operators, one-dimensional applications, angular momentum, hydrogen and approximation methods. The University of São Paulo Quantum Mechanics I catalogue similarly covers formal structure, oscillator, central fields, spin, identical particles and perturbation.

The present course preserves this international core and extends it toward chemistry using the uploaded Grinter, Mueller, Piela and Roos texts: covalent bonding, molecular spectroscopy, Hartree–Fock, basis sets, electron correlation and multiconfigurational quantum chemistry.
