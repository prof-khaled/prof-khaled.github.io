# International benchmarking report

The website architecture was benchmarked against established university general-chemistry practice rather than copied from a single syllabus. MIT OpenCourseWare 5.111 was reviewed for the expected emphasis on molecular reasoning, quantitative problem solving, lecture–recitation integration, and cumulative assessment. OpenStax Chemistry 2e was used as an open educational cross-check for broad first-year scope. ChemCollective was reviewed for scenario-based and virtual-laboratory approaches. The three uploaded textbooks provide the controlling academic content and alternative organizations.

## Synthesized design decisions

- Two-term, first-year course structure with lecture, tutorial, laboratory, and independent study.
- Explicit learning outcomes connecting models, calculations, evidence, and laboratory practice.
- Cumulative assessment rather than isolated chapter recall.
- Safety, uncertainty, data integrity, environmental responsibility, and responsible digital-tool use.
- Atoms-first primary sequence, with conventional-sequence cross-links supplied by supporting texts.
- Fully bilingual delivery and a standalone offline-capable implementation.
