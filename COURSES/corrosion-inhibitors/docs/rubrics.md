# Rubrics

## Laboratory and Monitoring Report — 100 marks

| Criterion | Marks | Excellent evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Experimental design and controls | 15 | Representative conditions, blank, replication, stated variables |
| Calculations and units | 20 | Correct equations, units, uncertainty, traceable steps |
| Data quality and visualization | 15 | Clear tables/graphs, measured vs calculated labels, raw-data traceability |
| Mechanistic interpretation | 20 | Integrates mass loss, electrochemistry, surface and process evidence |
| Localized-corrosion assessment | 10 | Pit/crevice evidence and limitations addressed |
| Safety and environmental practice | 10 | Risk, waste, and current verification requirements |
| Bilingual technical communication | 10 | Accurate, readable, equivalent Arabic and English summaries |

## Capstone Project — 100 marks

| Criterion | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Mechanism and design basis | 20 |
| Candidate screening and mandatory criteria | 10 |
| Qualification evidence | 20 |
| Formulation, dose, and application | 15 |
| Monitoring, alarms, and failure response | 15 |
| Safety, environment, and waste | 10 |
| Economics and implementation | 5 |
| Bilingual evidence-based communication | 5 |

## General performance descriptors

- **Outstanding:** accurate, integrated, quantitative, evidence-based, limitations explicit.
- **Competent:** mostly correct with minor gaps; viable engineering recommendation.
- **Developing:** partial understanding, inconsistent calculations, weak integration.
- **Insufficient:** incorrect mechanisms, unsupported claims, unsafe or unmonitored design.
