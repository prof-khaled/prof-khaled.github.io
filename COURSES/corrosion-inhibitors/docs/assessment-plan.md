# Assessment Plan

| Assessment | Mode | Weight | ILO coverage | Feedback | Integrity/accessibility controls |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Module quizzes | Individual | 10% | 1–9, 11 | Immediate explanations | Two attempts; keyboard-accessible; bilingual |
| Quantitative problem sets | Individual | 10% | 2, 3, 7, 8 | Annotated solutions | Calculation steps and units required |
| Laboratory and monitoring report | Individual/team data, individual report | 15% | 2, 6, 7, 11, 12 | Rubric and data conference | Raw data retained; accessible alternatives |
| Midterm examination | Individual | 20% | 1–5, 7 | Model answers and analytics | Controlled equation sheet; accommodations |
| Capstone project | Team design, individual defence | 25% | 4–12 | Staged feedback and rubric | Source log, AI declaration, individual defence |
| Final examination | Individual | 20% | All | Marking guide and review | Accessible format; consistent calculator policy |

Total = **100%**.

## Responsible AI requirement

AI-supported language editing or brainstorming must be declared. Students remain responsible for calculations, source verification, data integrity, and engineering recommendations. Fabricated data or citations are prohibited.
