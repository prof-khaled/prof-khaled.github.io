# Version 8 — Refined Palette and Two-Line Compact Navigation

## Design
- Preserves the navy, blue, gold and warm paper palette from the supplied reference homepage.
- Strengthens text contrast on light and dark sections.
- Uses consistent white cards and muted body text.

## Navigation
- Desktop navigation is arranged as a compact 4 × 2 grid.
- All eight top-level navigation/tool groups remain visible in exactly two rows.
- At 1120px and below, the navigation changes to an accessible collapsible menu instead of wrapping unpredictably.
- Dropdowns remain available on desktop and mobile.
