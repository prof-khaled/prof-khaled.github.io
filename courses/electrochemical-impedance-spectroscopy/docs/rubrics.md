# Rubrics

## Laboratory and notebook portfolio — 100 points

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Scientific question and preparation | 10 | Bounded hypothesis, source-based method, safety plan |
| Cell, instrument, calibration, and metadata | 20 | Geometry, connections, ranges, dummy-cell verification |
| Raw data and quality control | 20 | Complete files, linearity, stability, repeatability, artifact checks |
| Graphs and calculations | 15 | Axes, units, frequency direction, equations, uncertainty |
| Modeling and residual analysis | 20 | Competing models, weighting, bounds, residuals, identifiability |
| Interpretation and independent evidence | 10 | Mechanistic restraint and supporting measurement |
| Communication and reproducibility | 5 | Bilingual clarity, citations, file organization |

## Capstone — 100 points

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Question, material, operating state, and decision need | 15 |
| Measurement design and validation | 20 |
| Analysis, CNLS, uncertainty, and model alternatives | 25 |
| Application evidence and independent methods | 15 |
| Bounded decision, limitations, and next experiment | 15 |
| Professional bilingual presentation and defense | 10 |

### Performance descriptors
- **Excellent:** technically correct, reproducible, critical, uncertainty-aware, and independently supported.
- **Competent:** correct main method and calculations with minor omissions.
- **Developing:** partially correct but weak validation, units, residual analysis, or evidence.
- **Insufficient:** invalid data used uncritically, unsupported circuit assignments, missing raw data, or unsafe design.
