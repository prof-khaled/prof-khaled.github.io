# Quality-Assurance Report

## Academic completeness
- 12 modules cover foundations, mathematics, ideal and nonideal elements, graphical interpretation, interfaces, transport, instrumentation, validation, fitting, and major applications.
- Primary textbook controls the sequence; supporting texts expand practical and advanced topics.
- Every module includes outcomes, rationale, prerequisites, explanatory concepts, equations, a worked example, a guided problem, a practical activity, misconceptions, feedback, and source notes.

## Equations
- Equations are used for genuine instructional purposes.
- Symbols, units, assumptions, and cautions are explained in surrounding text.
- The interactive simulator uses the displayed ideal or semi-empirical equations and labels output as simulation.

## Visuals
- All static visuals are original SVGs.
- Captions identify them as conceptual and cite the source relationship.
- Canvas graphs contain axes and units; frequency direction is explained.
- No fabricated measurement is presented as experimental.

## Assessment

- ILOs are taught, practised, and assessed.
- Practical and modeling tasks require raw data, validation, residuals, uncertainty, and independent evidence.
