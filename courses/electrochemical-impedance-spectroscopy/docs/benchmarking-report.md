## Benchmark conclusion

The course adopts a rigorous sequence of electrochemical foundations, mathematical tools, instrumentation, practical experiments, data validation, modeling, and applications. It adds a stronger dedicated treatment of Kramers–Kronig validation, CNLS identifiability, bilingual technical communication, and reproducible data practice than is visible in most broad electrochemistry catalog descriptions.
