# Visual research and use report

The attached course prompt prioritizes genuine scientific visuals and authorizes reuse/adaptation of uploaded textbook material with citation. The visual strategy therefore uses uploaded textbook figures when they directly clarify a concept, and original course redraws/graphs when a cleaner explanation is preferable. No unlicensed external internet image is reproduced.

Textbook figures used include the black-body spectrum and photoelectric apparatus from the uploaded Quantum Physics excerpt, interference from Cox, p-orbital angular forms and H2 antibonding density from Grinter, and the CASSCF/RASSCF partition from Roos et al. The visual asset manifest records exact page/figure metadata.
