# Visual Research and Use Report

## Policy applied

The supplied course master prompt requires scientifically meaningful visuals, source/page/figure attribution, alt text, and a verified visual manifest. It also authorizes reproduction/adaptation of the **uploaded** course materials with attribution. Accordingly, this course favors two visual classes:

1. **Uploaded-textbook figures** used only where they directly teach the surrounding concept.
2. **Original course diagrams/graphs** redrawn from cited scientific relationships when a clearer or more accessible teaching visual is preferable.

No third-party internet artwork has been copied into the site.

## Original educational visuals

The SVGs for SCF, force-field terms, basis-set hierarchy, DFT ladder, optimization, MD, reaction path, QM/MM and reproducible workflow are original conceptual illustrations. They do not represent measured data. Their captions identify the scientific source relationships.
