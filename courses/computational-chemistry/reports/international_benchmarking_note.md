# International benchmarking note

This course is structured as an advanced undergraduate / introductory postgraduate computational chemistry course with a strong laboratory and reproducibility component. The attached textbooks themselves describe one- and two-semester curricular roles. The final local mapping should be checked against the institution's approved programme specification before deployment.

Benchmark principles applied in the design:

- Foundation in molecular models, potential-energy surfaces and quantum mechanics before black-box software use.
- Separate coverage of molecular mechanics/sampling and electronic-structure methods.
- Explicit Hartree–Fock, basis-set, correlation and DFT foundations rather than software-only training.
- Practical workflows for optimization, frequencies, reaction paths, spectroscopy and solvation.
- Current research practice: reproducibility, method validation, high-throughput records and uncertainty.
- Capstone requiring an end-to-end computational argument, source/citation governance and reproducible artifacts.

**Verification requirement:** institutional contact hours, local software availability, assessment regulations and programme-level ILO mapping remain provisional.
