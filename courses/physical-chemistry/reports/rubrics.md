# Rubrics / سلالم التقييم

## Quantitative problem
20% principle/equation selection; 20% assumptions/setup; 30% algebra/substitution/units; 15% numerical accuracy/significant figures; 15% interpretation/plausibility.

## Laboratory/computational report
15% purpose/theory; 20% method/calibration/safety; 20% data quality/uncertainty; 20% calculations/visualization; 20% discussion; 5% citation/reproducibility.

## Conceptual response
Reward precise definitions, causal reasoning, model/equation or graph support where relevant, explicit assumptions and limitations, and cross-scale connections.
