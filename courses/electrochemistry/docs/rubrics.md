# Rubrics / معايير التصحيح

## Laboratory portfolio (100 marks)
- Preparation and scientific rationale: 10
- Cell design, calibration, controls, and safety: 15
- Raw data, metadata, and traceability: 15
- Calculations, units, and uncertainty: 20
- Interpretation and comparison with models: 20
- Limitations, waste, ethics, and responsible AI disclosure: 10
- Bilingual technical communication and citations: 10

## Data-analysis project (100 marks)
- Reproducible files and data provenance: 15
- Validation and preprocessing: 15
- Model or method selection: 20
- Quantitative analysis and uncertainty: 25
- Alternative explanations and evidence-bounded conclusion: 15
- Communication, figures, references, and AI disclosure: 10

## Technology design project (100 marks)
- Requirements and reaction scheme: 15
- Thermodynamic, kinetic, and transport analysis: 25
- Cell/material/process design: 20
- Performance, monitoring, and validation plan: 15
- Safety, environmental, lifecycle, and resource analysis: 15
- Bilingual defense and responses to questions: 10
