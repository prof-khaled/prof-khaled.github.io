# International Benchmarking Report

The course was benchmarked conceptually against common upper-undergraduate and introductory postgraduate corrosion-engineering expectations:

- electrochemical thermodynamics and kinetics;
- mixed-potential and polarization interpretation;
- adsorption and inhibitor chemistry;
- passivation and localized corrosion;
- mass-loss and electrochemical evaluation;
- industrial dosing and monitoring;
- safety, environmental performance, and engineering decision-making;
- an integrative design project.

The website emphasizes inhibitor-specific depth rather than reproducing a general corrosion course. It uses a 12-module sequence, a 15-week plan, six practical activities, quantitative calculators, immediate-feedback quizzes, a midterm, a cumulative final blueprint, and a capstone management design.

Formal accreditation mapping remains institution-specific and is listed in the assumptions register.
