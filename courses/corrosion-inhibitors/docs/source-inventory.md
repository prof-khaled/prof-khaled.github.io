# Source Inventory

## PT1 — Primary textbook

**V. S. Sastri. _Corrosion Inhibitors: Principles and Applications_. Wiley, 1998.**

Role: controls core sequence, terminology, purpose, economics, safety, adsorption, electrochemical principles, chemical and quantum aspects, industrial applications, and surface analysis.

## ST1 — Supporting textbook

**V. S. Sastri. _Green Corrosion Inhibitors: Theory and Practice_. Wiley, 2011.**

Role: expands electrochemical foundations, adsorption, monitoring, industrial applications, environmental testing, toxicity, biodegradation, rare-earth compounds, and oleochemicals.

## ST2 — Historical industrial guide

**Ernest W. Flick. _Corrosion Inhibitors: An Industrial Guide_, 2nd ed. Noyes Publications, 1993.**

Role: historical industrial product families, physical forms, application methods, sectors, and formulation context. It is not treated as a current purchasing guide. Current supplier and safety information must be verified.

## MP1 — Course-design specification

**General Master Prompt — Creating a Complete Bilingual University Course Website.**

Role: website architecture, bilingual standard, accessibility, assessment, source governance, evidence files, and quality assurance.
