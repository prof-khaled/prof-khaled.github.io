# Visual research and design report

The uploaded books were examined for apparatus concepts, electrochemical diagrams, characteristic plots and technique relationships. To avoid reproducing publisher artwork and to maintain consistent bilingual accessibility, the website uses newly drawn SVG schematics. The visual set covers corrosion cells, double layers, three-electrode control, Pourbaix and mixed-potential concepts, passivity, LPR, EIS, rotating disks, current distributions, noise, local scanning, Kelvin probe, microcells, photoelectrochemistry/EQCM and method selection.

All drawings are conceptual and contain no measured data. Axis shapes and circuit relationships are educational idealizations grounded in the cited chapters.
